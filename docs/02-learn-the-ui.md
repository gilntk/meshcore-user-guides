# Learn the UI {#learn-the-ui}

Once your radio is set up, the MeshCore app opens to its main screen. This
chapter is a quick tour of what you see there and what each part is for. The
rest of this guide uses the names introduced here.

## The main screen {#main-screen}

::::::: columns
::: {.column width="54%"}
### Header {#header}

The row across the top (1) shows your radio's name and battery level, and holds
controls that act on your radio and the app as a whole. The header appears
on the Contacts, Channels, and Map screens. It doesn't appear on a contact's
details screen or when you open a channel.

### Search bar {#search-bar}

The search bar (2) finds what you're looking for in the current screen's
content area.

### Content area {#content-area}

The middle of the screen (3) shows the items and information for whichever
screen you've chosen in the tab bar: your contacts, your channels, or the
map.

### Tab bar {#tab-bar}

The tab bar (4) at the bottom switches between the Contacts, Channels, and Map
screens. A number on a tab shows how many unread messages are waiting there.
:::

### Contacts screen {#contacts-screen}

::::::: columns
::: {.column width="60%"}
The Contacts screen lists your contacts. That includes contacts added
automatically when your radio hears their advert, as well as the ones you've
added yourself.

The filter button (a funnel) at the right end of the search bar narrows the
list, with options that apply to contacts.

Each row is one contact:

- The icon on the left shows the contact's type.
- Tap anywhere on the row to open the contact's details screen.
- Tap the row menu (⋮) at the right end of the row to open a quick actions
  menu for that contact.

### Channels screen {#channels-screen}

::::::: columns
::: {.column width="60%"}
The Channels screen lists the channels you've added.

The filter button in the search bar works the same way as on the Contacts
screen, with options that apply to channels.

Each row is one channel:

- The icon on the left shows the channel's type.
- Tap anywhere on the row to open the channel.
- Tap the row menu (⋮) at the right end of the row to open a quick actions
  menu for that channel.

### Map screen {#map-screen}

::::::: columns
::: {.column width="60%"}
The Map screen shows where your contacts are. A contact appears on the map
only if a location has been set for it. Contacts without one are still on
the Contacts screen; they just aren't placed on the map.

The map works like other phone maps: drag to move around, and pinch to zoom
in and out.

When you zoom out far enough that contacts would overlap, the map groups
them into **clusters**. A cluster is a circle with a number showing how many
contacts it holds. Tap a cluster to zoom in on it. As you zoom in, clusters
split into smaller clusters and then into individual contacts, each shown
with its type icon, such as a tower for a repeater.

On this screen the search bar has no filter button and only searches
contact names. The filter is one of the map controls, the round buttons
along the right edge:

- **Filter** (funnel): choose which contacts appear on the map.
- **Layers** (stacked squares): choose the base map.
- **Refresh** (circular arrow): zoom the map out.

## Interact with the MeshCore app {#interact}

The MeshCore app works like most phone apps, with the standard iOS and
Android gestures. This guide uses these terms for them:

| Gesture | Meaning |
|---|---|
| tap | Touch an item briefly. |
| touch and hold | Touch an item and keep your finger still until a menu or selection appears. |
| swipe | Slide your finger across the screen. |
| drag | Touch the screen and move your finger without lifting it, for example to move around a map. |
| pinch | Touch the screen with two fingers and move them together to zoom out, or apart to zoom in. |

Touching and holding an item often shows extra options. Tap a text field,
such as the search bar, to bring up your phone's keyboard.
